Robotics · UCLA RoMeLa

Mingzhang Zhu

朱明彰 / Norman

I’m a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering at UCLA, working in the Robotics & Mechanisms Laboratory (RoMeLa) with Prof. Dennis Hong.

I previously worked at Dyna Robotics as a hardware research intern, focusing on immersive dexterous hand teleoperation.

I design robotic systems that connect capable hardware with intelligent motion. My research focuses on dexterous robotic hands, human–robot interfaces, mechanism design, and dynamic humanoid locomotion.

Previously, I received my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering and Robotics from Worcester Polytechnic Institute in 2021.
